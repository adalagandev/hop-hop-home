# How to Play — Hop-Hop-Home

Help a friendly animal find its way home! You don't steer the animal as it goes — you **plan every step first**, then watch it follow your plan. It's a fun way to learn to think ahead, like a real programmer.

Made for kids aged about 5 and up. A grown-up can help with the first round.

---

## Your goal
Get the animal to its **home spot** — the square that glows green.

## Meet the animals
Every game picks one of five worlds at random:
- **Bunny Meadow** — help Nibbles the bunny hop to the burrow.
- **Turtle Cove** — help Shelly the turtle get back to the tide pool.
- **Orca Passage** — help Kai the orca swim to the open bay.
- **Koala Grove** — help Bramble the koala reach the gum tree.
- **Penguin Floes** — help Pip the penguin swim to the big iceberg.

Tap the wooden sign at the top to see what your animal needs to do. Tap it again to see the world's name.

---

## The buttons
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Blue curly arrow (left)** | Turn to the left |
| **Yellow arrow** | Take one step forward |
| **Blue curly arrow (right)** | Turn to the right |
| **Pink jump arrow** | Jump over the next square and land on the one after it |
| **Pink spin arrow** | Turtle only (instead of jump): Shelly spins in place and breaks a soft golden square in front in one go |
| **GO!** | Start the adventure! |

Your animal always starts in the **bottom-left corner**, looking up or to the right.

Watch which way your animal is looking — "forward" means the way it's facing.

## Making a plan
1. Tap the buttons to add steps. They appear one by one in the **Adventure Plan** on the right.
2. Made a mistake? **Tap a step in the plan** to take it out, or tap the red **⟲** by the plan to start the plan again.
3. **Light green boxes** in the plan show the fewest steps you need before you can tap GO.
4. You can plan up to **8 steps**.
5. When your plan is long enough, the **GO!** button turns green. Tap it!

## Watching your plan
- First, little **arrows** show the path your plan will take. It's a sneak peek!
- Then your animal follows the plan one step at a time. The step it's doing grows bigger in the plan.
- If your animal doesn't make it home, don't worry — it waits where it stopped. Just plan the next steps from there.

---

## Things in the way
- **Rocks, coral, icebergs, stumps and islets** can't be walked through. Bump into one and it flashes red, and your animal wiggles.
- **Golden squares with a dashed ring** are *soft*. Bump into one **3 times** and it pops! The little dots on it show how many bumps are left.
- Before a jump, a **pink dashed line** shows where your animal will land.
- **Jumping** takes your animal over anything, but it has to land on an empty square. You can't land on something in the way, or off the edge of the board.
- The edge of the board is a wall too.
- Tap any square just for fun: things on it do a little dance, and empty squares blink 3 times.

## Winning
When your animal reaches home, the board sparkles, everyone dances, and you get a big **YAY!** Then a brand-new world with a different animal appears.

---

## Settings (gear button ⚙)
- **Pick a world**: choose which animal you'd like to play.
- **Board size**: 4 × 4 (easy), 10 × 5 (a long, wide trail) or 6 × 6 (tricky).
- **Sound**: turn it on or off.
- Tap **Puzzle Mode** next to the sign to switch to puzzle play (the button then says **Find Home Mode** — tap it to go back to the normal home trip). In Puzzle Mode, a purple puzzle piece hops one square every 3 steps. Its dots show how many steps are left before it moves. Tap it to see a dashed line to where it will go next. Catch it to win! Puzzle mode plays in **Koala Grove** on the **10 × 5** board. Every piece you catch fills in one more square of the koala family picture (9 pieces in all), and the picture pops up right on the game screen with the new piece appearing. Tap **Next round** to keep going. When the last piece goes in, the finished picture spins and shines, gets saved to your **Koala Album** (the gold photo button next to the puzzle button), and the puzzle count goes back to 0 for a new picture. Collect all 5 koala pictures to fill the album! Tap the purple puzzle button next to **GO!** to see your picture so far.
- Tap **Close** or anywhere outside the settings to go back.

The red **⟲** at the corner of the board gives you a new puzzle.

## Tips for young adventurers
- Before you tap anything, point to the path with your finger.
- Say each step out loud: "turn, step, step, jump!"
- Soft golden squares can be a shortcut, but they take 3 bumps. Sometimes going around is faster.
- Jumping is a great way to hop over a rock.

Works on computers, tablets and phones.
